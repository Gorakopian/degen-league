How it works

  • Pick 5 traders from the market with a budget of 100 credits. Stars cost more, so find cheap sleepers.
  • Choose a captain. Their score counts double.
  • Teams lock Monday 00:00 UTC. You pick for next week; you can change your team until it locks.
  • Score: each trader's % return that week on coins they bought that week, read straight from their wallet. Sold coins count what they got back; coins still held count at today's price.
  • Your points = the 5 returns added up, captain twice. Scores update through the week.
  • A trader who spends less than 1 SOL in the week scores 0.
  • Prices move every week: big winners get more expensive, cold traders get cheaper.
  • Free to play. Signing in only proves you own your wallet. It never moves funds.
  • Traders come from the public Kolscan leaderboard. Just for fun, not financial advice.
